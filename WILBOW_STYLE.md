Notes on how Wilbow writes and how he likes to be asked.

Transcription mishears words, especially "ohm" ("Gnome was just voice chat mistaking ohm", 10:26 PM). Quote him verbatim in the logs, typos included, but act on what he meant; if a word makes no sense, consider a mishearing and check.

He often answers in chat instead of on the card; treat that as the answer if it fits.

5. **Diagrams when they help** (11:12 PM Oct 24): "Let's use diagrams when we need them too". Use diagrams in chat or on the Page when a picture explains a system better than text. Cards for quick picks, the Page for big material.
