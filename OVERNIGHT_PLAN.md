## Chunks (each one is a test APK)
1. **v0.8a-1 Persistence foundation (V08 H) + O1 counters.** Changes are saved per place as small deltas, saves happen while idle, a versioned registry lets future things (engines, effects, glyphs) save without breaking old saves, v0.7 saves migrate, and every changed world stays reachable. Per-ohm counters (watched, taps, catches, plays, offspring) start recording. Test: change things, close the app, reopen, and everything is still there; long sessions stay smooth.
2. **v0.8a-2 Two-stroke glyphs (O6).** Undo = hook + `<` or `>`. "?" = hook + point under it, which calls the guide in (the full relations view comes later). A hook alone becomes a normal material rod (3:12 answer, Q103). Test: draw both, and check that hook-shaped strokes no longer undo by accident.
3. **v0.8a-3 Existing systems (V08 A–D).** A: a closed shape plus a rest becomes an effect item. B: ohms carry water. C: light regional keys. D: jam-tuned strings from the joined ohms' notes.
3b. **v0.8a-3b Drawn instruments (V08 P).** Needs H (saved voices and ensembles) and D (jam-tuned snapping). Drag a rod's end (or a blob's edge) to move it, and length or size sets the pitch by string, bar and drum physics, snapped to the nearby jam's key, else the valley's or the world's. Every drawn shape gets its own seeded voice from all 9 engines plus the physical models. Ohms play shapes their own way (strike, pluck, bow, sing-to, perch). Ensembles: drone rod, marimba row, harp frame, drum circle. Played shapes partly pull nearby jams' tempo and melody (capped at 25%, fading after they stop, baton wins). Test: lengthen a rod and hear it drop through the key; build a marimba row and a drum circle and watch ohms play them; play steadily beside a jam and hear it lean in, then drift back.
3c. **v0.8a-3c Room in the jam (V08 Q).** Each ohm feels how much of the jam it fills, in time (which beats and subdivisions) and in pitch (which octaves and bands, how loud), and makes room or fills gaps: thins out, goes softer, moves an octave, shifts to empty beats (interlocking like hocket), or fills an empty band. Show-offs keep more room, shy ones step back first, and skilled ohms pick the best move. Your strings and drawn shapes count as a voice the ohms make room for. The baton wins. Once per bar on screen, nothing off screen. Test: start a 6-ohm jam and hear it go from muddy to clear within a few bars; add and remove ohms and hear the others rearrange.
4. **v0.8a-4 Music pattern library (replaces "Missing v0.7 cultures", Wilbow 3:45 AM).** Many traditions to use, mix and match, designed in PATTERN_LIBRARY.md by Resonance Music Theory; includes the four missing v0.7 traditions (overtone/throat singing, Persian dastgah, rumba clave with samba partido-alto, jazz ii-V-I with extended chords). Design landed 4:09 AM (PATTERN_LIBRARY.md, 30 traditions). Built in phases as testable APKs: 4A make existing cultures clearly audible + theory bug fixes; 4B library file + the four missing traditions; 4C mix-and-match engine; 4D remaining traditions in batches + perf test. Notes and playing style only; text-free (glyph, colour, signature motif).
