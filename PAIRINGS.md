# The 16 symbiotic pairing ideas (original text) and where each stands (v0.6.1)

The text of each idea is the exact original, copied from PAIRINGS_ORIGINAL.md. The status lines below each one are ours.
Separate from the eight approved earlier (echo feeding, crystal farming, glitch cleaners, ember keepers, weather charging,
reverb burrows, sound borrowing, style pollinators). Everything is learned behaviour; the genome never changes (mutation 0).

**Creature with creature**

1. **Carrier and rider.** A small high-pitched creature rides a big low one, so together they cover the full range. The rider gets to travel, and the carrier gets a melody on top.
   - **v0.6, built, near home (no travel):** a small high ohm whose home is within 200 px of a big low drone's home settles beside it (off-screen, home moved at most 120 px). It moves toward the carrier's key and tempo and plays on top; the carrier gains a little energy while the rider plays. A short bond sign shows when they pair. The 'rider travels' part is left out.
   - **v0.6.1, reviewed and left as built:** riding off with the carrier would take the rider away from home, and ohms must stay near home. The near-home version above stays.
2. **Timekeeper and wanderer.** A steady percussive creature anchors a free, rubato soloist, which keeps the beat and lets the soloist drift. The soloist's ideas give the drummer variations to play.
   - Built v0.4b: a lone pitched ohm bonds to a nearby drummer and locks to its tempo.
   - **v0.5c, built:** the soloist (a lone pitched ohm bonded to a live drummer within ~830 px) plays rubato: its inner notes bend inside the bar (up to ~2/3 of a step, more for flexible ohms), while the downbeat and the bar length stay exact. Every 4th bar the drummer moves or accents at most 2 of its own hits onto the soloist's onsets (it never adds notes). A short dotted link shows it.
3. **Breath and body.** A wind-blowing creature plays through a creature with a hollow horn or sac, so neither can make that sound alone. They seek each other out.
   - Built v0.4b: a blower breathes on a drum-belly ohm, and the body's next bar sounds out.
   - **v0.5c, built:** the joint voice. At most every 6 s, a puff sounds one soft, sustained note through the body's own resonator with a blown, breathy exciter, on the body's tonic and next beat. The crowd cap still applies. A link fx shows it.
4. **Tuner and drifter.** A creature with exact intonation gradually pulls slightly out-of-tune creatures into tune when near them, and the drifters give it microtonal colour it can borrow.
   - Built v0.4b: tonic snap when more than 50 cents off.
   - **v0.5b:** a gradual fine-tuning pull (30% per second when 3-50 cents off), and the tuner borrows the drifter's offset as microtonal colour (half of it, on its 4th degree).
5. **Hearing and seeing.** Blind and deaf creatures pair up. The deaf one feels rhythm through vibration and the blind one hears pitch, and together they jam perfectly.
   - **v0.6, built:** a deaf ohm and a blind ohm within 320 px pair up. The deaf one takes the blind one's beat (tempo pull, phase lock once within 1 bpm), and the blind one is pulled toward the deaf one's key. A short bond sign shows when they pair.
6. **Cleaner and hoarder.** One creature gathers worn-out effect items from a hoarder's pile and restores them, and the hoarder gives it some in return.
   - **v0.6, built:** a calm, non-percussive ohm within 260 px of a hoarder's worn item (wear 0.25 or more) restores it a little each second (less wear, more charge). When it is fully restored and neither ohm is on screen, the hoarder hands it over.
7. **Harmony nest.** A chord-playing colonial creature hosts smaller creatures inside its cluster, so they sing within its chord and are sheltered from clashes.
   - Built v0.4b (our version): the ground nest of a coherent jam, which keeps its key.
   - **v0.5c, built:** the cluster chord ohm. A pitched ohm with a cluster body offers its bar's chord (kept for 8 s). A lone pitched ohm within 240 px becomes its guest: its notes snap to the nearest chord tone in its own octave, and it drifts in to sit about 60 px beside the host (inside its home leash). A dotted link shows it.
8. **Call and echo twins.** Two creatures settle into a permanent call-and-answer bond, where one always phrases and the other always answers in a transformed way.
   - Built v0.4b; v0.5a added echoes inside conversation turns.

**Creature with plants and ground**

9. **Garden tenders.** Creatures sing to a plant to shape its growth in their style, and the plant's hum gives them a drone to play over.
   - Built v0.5a: gardeners' music counts double, and they perk up when the plant flowers.
   - **v0.5b:** the gardeners' style shapes the growth (lively, hard-played singing grows a wilder plant; gentle singing a calmer one). A tended plant hums a quiet drone on its root note every 10 s.
10. **Root resonance.** Creatures sit on plant roots that carry their sound across the area to distant creatures, a kind of underground telephone.
    - **v0.5b, built:** an ohm within 110 px of a plant's base is heard by ohms sitting by other plants up to 2600 px away. A dotted link shows it.
11. **Seed spreaders.** Creatures carry jam seeds in their appendages and plant them wherever they go next, spreading songs around the planet.
    - Built v0.5a: one jam member carries the jam seed home and plants it.
    - **v0.5b seed fix:** a blocked carrier plants the seed where it stands near home, or keeps it and tries again.
    - **v0.5c, built:** onward carrying, near home. A grown jam plant (maturity 0.5 or more) hands a copy of its seed to a free ohm passing within 200 px whose home is 120-420 px away. That ohm plants it 60-140 px from its own home, so songs spread hop by hop and never by long trips. Limits: up to 4 generations, 3 seedlings per plant, one hand-off per plant per 15 min (kept in the save), and the plant cap (24). A short link shows the hand-off.
    - Extra (ours, kept and labelled as an extra): the plant chorus, where lone ohms by a replaying jam plant drift into its tempo.
12. **Mineral grinders.** Percussive creatures break down ringing ground into fine dust that softens and warms the area, which shy creatures prefer.
    - **v0.6, built:** a percussive ohm playing within 200 px of a ringing crystal raises a dust patch there (16 patches at most; it fades slowly). Dust softens high partials in that spot, and shy ohms move their home onto a dust patch 40-180 px away (off-screen, at most 120 px). The dust shows as 14 tiny drawn specks per patch in view, with no particles.
13. **Wind farmers.** Wind-blowing creatures clear pathways that send wind through hollow formations, keeping a natural drone going that others tune to.
    - Built v0.4b: tending makes a wind rock or reed bed ring longer and richer.
    - **v0.5b:** a well-farmed feature (level 0.4 or above) keeps a quiet, lasting drone going in still air, rooted on the nearest ohm's tonic.
14. **Dew collectors.** Creatures that gather moisture keep an area damp, which muffles harshness and lets fragile, quiet creatures settle there.
    - Built v0.4b: dew carrying.
    - **v0.5b:** damp ground absorbs the high partials (harshness muffled), and quiet or shy ohms find damp ground more comfortable. The intermittent #14 test failure is fixed.

**Wider systems**

15. **Food web of sound.** Some creatures feed on the leftover decay of others' notes, so an area full of long reverb tails supports more of them. Silence starves them.
    - **v0.6, built:** hungry, non-percussive ohms gain a little energy from other ohms' long notes (0.6 s or longer) within 320 px. After 12 s of quiet they take a short rest; they never starve.
16. **Population balance.** Too many of one instrument type in an area makes the music muddy, which pushes some of them to move away, so a natural orchestra forms over time.
    - **v0.6, built inside the home leash:** when three or more ohms with the same instrument type are within 250 px, one of them moves its home up to 120 px away from the group (off-screen, at most one move a minute, once per ohm). The 190 px leash is never left.
    - **v0.6.1, reviewed and left as built:** moving beyond the leash would break the rule that ohms stay near home. The leashed version above stays.
